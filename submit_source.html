Submission Guidelines

  • Submit accurate and reliable sources from credible, independent organizations.
  • Link to a specific bill, vote, or claim on LACE.
  • Include enough detail for moderators to verify the information.
  • Avoid partisan opinion pieces, blog posts, or unverified content.
  • All submissions are reviewed before being published.

Acceptable Source Examples

    News Articlese.g., Reuters, AP, BBC, local news outlets
  • 🏛
    Government Documentse.g., bill text, committee reports, hearing transcripts
  • 📊
    Official Datae.g., Census, BLS, CBO, state government data
  • ▶️
    Official Videose.g., committee hearings, floor sessions (C-SPAN, state legislature channels)
